What this site is

The XAUUSD Quantum 5.0 engine — the Pine Master at build v21 — ported line for line to Python and run on free market data by a scheduled GitHub Actions job. The job downloads data, runs the engine on every timeframe, backtests all three arms, and publishes the results here as static JSON. The page itself makes no decisions. It shows what the engine concluded, and how it got there.

How it runs

  1. Download (every 15 minutes while gold trades): XAU/USD price, COMEX gold futures (GC) volume, DXY, US 10-year, EURUSD, silver, S&P 500, VIX (Yahoo Finance); US 2-year and 10-year real yield (FRED); weekly Commitments of Traders for gold (CFTC). Each download is merged into a store on the market-data branch, so the history grows beyond the 60 days Yahoo serves for 5- and 15-minute bars.
  2. Engine: on every confirmed bar: trend, regime, sessions, structure (BOS / CHoCH / MSS), displacement, order blocks, fair value gaps, liquidity pools and sweeps, macro votes and correlations, the weighted evidence composite, the historical-analog engine and its Platt calibration, the trade plan, trade quality, the vetoes and the account-state risk tracker.
  3. Backtest of the three arms: treatment (the live gate), control (the pre-Q5.5 gates) and challenger (pre-registered hypothesis H1: the fast trend term EMA20 in the entry gate).
  4. Publish to GitHub Pages; alerts go out for newly confirmed signals (ntfy / Telegram / Discord, optional).

Where it deliberately differs from the Pine version

These are removals of TradingView limitations, not tuning. None of them was chosen by looking at results.

  • D-01 · No repainting of higher-timeframe values. On historical bars TradingView returns a request.security(..., close[1]) value one HTF bar later than it does live. Here every bar uses the live behaviour, so the backtest is computed the way the signal is.
  • D-02 · Exact daylight-saving time. The Pine calendar rule switches European summer time up to four days early. Session windows gate entries, so this uses the IANA time-zone database instead.
  • D-03 / D-04 · Distinct analogs, whole buffer, every bar. Pine scans its analog buffer in chunks across several bars and keeps calibration counts across scan cycles with a 0.7 decay, so each analog is counted about 3.3 times and the N ≥ 30 bin gates are met by repetition. Here every count is a count of distinct analogs and the whole buffer is scanned on every bar. The thresholds are unchanged.
  • D-05 · COT without look-ahead. COT is aligned on its public release (Friday 15:30 New York), not on its Tuesday as-of date, and its percentile is taken over weeks rather than chart bars.
  • D-06 · Volume profile on every bar. Pine computes the value area only on the last bar (F-A07), so a historical plan could never use VAL/VAH/POC. Here it is computed for every bar.
  • D-07 · Real futures volume. Spot XAU/USD has no traded volume (TradingView's OANDA feed carries tick counts). Volume is taken from COMEX gold futures, bar for bar. A spot bar with no futures bar gets volume 0, and the count of such bars is shown in Diagnostics.
  • Open interest is weekly (CFTC), not daily. It is labelled wherever it appears.

What is honest to claim, and what is not

  • The historical window is contaminated. Quantum 5.x thresholds, weights and gates were selected while looking at gold's history. No re-slicing of that history — walk-forward, purged k-fold or ROLL — is out of sample.
  • The only out-of-sample evidence is the frozen forward holdout. It is pre-registered (start 2026-09-28 00:00 UTC; 1H decides; N ≥ 50 per arm; decision rules fixed in advance) and keyed on the configuration hash and a hash of the engine's source code, so every trade entered after the freeze was decided by parameters and code that could not have seen its outcome. Change any parameter or any engine file and the holdout restarts; the earlier one is kept in the manifest, its trades archived, and never merged. It is independent of the operator's own Excel log.
  • ROLL is not a holdout. The engine's rolling IS/ROLL split is a trailing slice that is redrawn every bar (F-037). It is labelled ROLL everywhere.
  • Scores are not probabilities. Confidence, trade quality, the bias composite and the what-if scores are heuristic 0–100 scores. The probability-shaped numbers are the calibrated P (after a Platt fit, marked unfitted before one exists), the race rates and the analog touch rates, each with its sample size.
  • No parameter here has been tuned against results. The defaults are the Pine Master's input defaults. That is a rule, not an accident (audit §9).

Limits you should know

  • Data is free, so it is delayed and occasionally wrong. Yahoo intraday data can arrive late, have gaps, or be revised. GitHub's scheduler can delay a run by several minutes, and occasionally skips one. Every source, its row count and last timestamp are in Diagnostics.
  • Price scale. If spot is unavailable the engine falls back to COMEX futures, which trade at a premium to spot. Set the MT5 offset (top bar) to your broker's difference. It shifts displayed prices only, as in Pine.
  • Parity with TradingView is not proven. The port follows the Pine source line by line and is covered by unit tests, but no TradingView chart was available to compare against. Differences D-01 to D-07 are intentional; anything else is a bug worth reporting.
  • Backtest fills are modelled, not observed. Entry is at the signal bar's close; a stop and a target in the same bar count as the stop (conservative); costs use the engine's session spread, slippage and commission model.

Glossary

  • TQ — trade quality, 0–100, direction-conditioned; entries below the regime-adjusted floor are vetoed.
  • Race EV — expected R of the TP1-versus-stop race from analog first-touch statistics, net of costs; a negative value vetoes entries.
  • P(long) / P(short) — Platt-calibrated probability of a bull / bear resolution, normalised across the two fits; below the minimum it vetoes entries once calibration is live.
  • ROLL — the trailing 20% of the analog buffer. A diagnostic, not a holdout.
  • Holdout — trades entered after the configuration freeze. The only unbiased evidence.
