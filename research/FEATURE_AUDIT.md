## Not tested (data does not exist here); never substituted

- true VWAP, volume profile / POC, relative volume (no traded volume; tick volume is not volume)
- bid / ask spread, tick-by-tick changes, price velocity / acceleration, aggressor proxies, intrabar sequence (no tick or quote data; not derived from OHLC)

- PCE (no dated list)

## Verdict

- **Only weekday and hour carry measurable information** (permutation +0.016 / +0.011).
  - Removing the TIME_NEWS family is the only removal that hurts (-0.016 AUC).
  - That information predicts weekend / holiday closures, not direction.
- **Every structure, liquidity, SMC, trend, oscillator, volatility and Markov feature adds nothing.**
  - Their permutation drops are at most about 0.002.
  - Removing whole families (liquidity, structure / SMC, momentum, trend, oscillator) improves the out-of-sample AUC.
- **No feature is carried into a strategy specification on this evidence** (operator rule: "If an indicator adds no
  predictive value, remove it from the proposed strategy specification").
