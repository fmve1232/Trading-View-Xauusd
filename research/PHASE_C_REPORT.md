## 5. Corrected model audit (gate C): B27 S3 and B29 C, separately

**The defect** (found by the audit after the registered runs): "top q" thresholds were quantiles of isotonic-calibrated probabilities. Those are a step function with about 20 values per window, so a nominal top 1% admitted 21–66% of the test rows. The registered small tails were never tested.

**The fix** (`627b2d2a3`, `b27.SELECT_ON = "raw"`):
- **Ranking.** Rank and select on the raw model score: the same order, without plateaus.
- **Threshold.** Still each training window's own (1 − q) quantile of its calibration rows, fixed out of sample.
- **Ties.** Every row at or above the threshold is selected, ties included, so the rule is deterministic.
- **Probabilities.** Probabilities, AUC, Brier and reliability still use the calibrated values.

Both studies were re-run in full from the same data and seeds.

**Audit findings after the registered runs, disclosed and fixed (all before this report, none changes a verdict):**

1. **The break-even comparator was too low.**
   - **Defect.** `break_even` was the break-even of the TP share of all trades. Beside the decided rate it understated by up to 9.8 points.
   - **Fix.** `break_even_decided` is exact: r > it if and only if expectancy > 0. It has a test.
   - **Impact.** Rows "above break-even" fall from 24 to 2 of 120 (`results/phasec/breakeven_audit.md`).
2. **Top-q selections were not q.** Fixed and re-run (§5).
3. **Order-entry resolution.** Fills inside a 15m bar are a worst case. The best case shows no hidden edge: 0 of 582 BH.
4. **B5c's tick reducer clamped mis-stamped ticks into the hour's last bar.** That is a silent shift; it was fixed before any data (A2, §13).

| Method | What it does |
|---|---|
| iid bootstrap | 2,000 paths of 250 trades drawn from the candidate's development trades |
| Nested bootstrap (95% interval of the iid figure) | 200 resamples of the trade pool × 200 paths |
| Block bootstrap | circular blocks of 10 consecutive trades, keeping clustered losses together |
| Actual order | the realised drawdown of the development series in chronological order |
| Regime-only | HIGH-volatility trades only, and LOW only |
| Cost stress | ADV − ENG added to every trade |
| 2026 | the 2026 trades |

⟪RISK_LINE⟫

**Sizing for any future validated edge:**
1. **Size from the validation lower bound, never from development.**
2. **Risk ≤ ¼ Kelly on that bound and ≤ 0.5% of the account per trade.**
3. **Stop for the day after two losses.**
4. **Halt the forward test** if the record falls below its registered random-entry band.
5. **Confirm the broker's contract size, commission and spreads first.**

**Kelly is negative for every grid pair** (A5), so the optimal size without an edge is zero.

## 11. Failure analysis: why nothing works

1. **The arithmetic.** Without directional information, TP-first = (SL − Δ) / (TP + SL), and the break-even is always higher. The grid measures exactly that.
2. **Costs set the sign.** Every pair loses about the cost of a round trip: a median $0.79 per oz.
3. **Development winners are selection noise.** Every frozen candidate regressed to its random rate or below on 2026, and the real results' extremes match the random walk's.
4. **Drift masquerades as an edge.**
   - Large-bracket BUYs and many BUY families look positive in 2020–22, a rising market.
   - Pooled with SELL, or checked on 2026, they lose.
   - Time-series momentum picked the month's direction more often than not, but its trades realised the neutral rate.
5. **The calendar masquerades as model skill.** Models "learn" the weekend close. On decided trades their AUC is 0.50.
6. **Resolution hides, it does not create.** The worst-case 15m rules make small brackets and large stop entries look worse. The best case shows no edge either.
7. **What the data cannot test.** Order flow, true volume, quotes and ticks, and multi-year 5m / 1m bars (§13, §14).

## 12. Recommended architecture (on this evidence)

```
 closed 15m bar  -->  default: NO TRADE / WAIT; show p0, break-even, cost and expectancy for the operator's TP / SL
          a signal may be shown as tradeable ONLY after, in order: registration -> null check -> development
          (BH, lower bound > matched random, n >= 300) -> frozen 2026 check (Holm) -> all six acceptance criteria
          -> sealed validation (operator approval) -> forward shadow record
```

- **Keep the site's NO TRADE verdict and the base-rate display** (display only).
- **Rank exits by expectancy per $ of risk, never by hit rate.**
- **Close or skip late-Friday entries.** That is the one calendar fact supported; it reduces weekend-gap exposure but makes nothing profitable.

## 13. Additional data research, kept separate from Phase C (gate A)

**B5c, Dukascopy top-of-book quote flow. REGISTERED, NOT RUN** (`registrations/B5C.json`). It needs the data, run on your laptop, because Dukascopy refuses GitHub's servers. Its results will never be pooled with Phase C.

- **Provenance:** Dukascopy SWFX best bid / ask with displayed liquidity, UTC hour files, record format and coverage. Raw ticks are never committed.
- **Execution:** the spread is a feature only; costs are B5's model. Every feature is quote-based; nothing is derived from OHLC.
- **Integrity gates (amendments A1 and A2, before any data).** Each is computed and written to `b5c_results_integrity.json` before any test:
  - correlation at every lag (−2..+2 bars), overall, by session and by year;
  - best lag and offset in minutes;
  - weekly open / close matching (time zone);
  - close- and open-side basis distributions;
  - missing spot bars;
  - duplicate-millisecond, crossed and out-of-hour ticks (counted and dropped, never clamped);
  - stale bars.
- **Quarantine and stop rules.**
  - Days with a median basis above 0.5% or a change correlation below 0.5 are quarantined: set to missing, never shifted.
  - The run stops on any offset, any session or year peaking off lag 0, more than 5% of days quarantined, more than 1% crossed quotes, more than 0.1% out-of-hour ticks, or weekly opens more than 15 minutes apart.
  - 14 tests cover them.
- **The lag-0 correlation is a preliminary gate, not proof.** Proof is the full integrity report. With no data supplied, **no alignment result exists yet.**
