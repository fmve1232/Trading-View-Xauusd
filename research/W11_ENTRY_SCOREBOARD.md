# W11: every entry plan on the operator's yardstick (2026-11-25)

**The operator's target:** from the entry, price reaches **+$10 at least** (preferably +$15 to +$20) in the alert's
direction **before the stop**, with a planned loss of at most $650 including costs.

**What this is:** every entry plan already measured on the development partition (24 Jan 2020 – 24 Jan 2023), placed on
that one yardstick. It reads the committed result files only (`research/tools/entry_scoreboard.py`). Nothing is
re-run, re-tuned or re-selected, no sealed partition is opened, and no trial is added to `research/trials.jsonl`. Each
row keeps its own study's stop, cost model and random control.

- **151 plans in all.** 66 plans against their own structural stop: B31b-W3 sweep reversals (20), W4 pullback / reversal / retest methods (15), W5 entry-price rules (21) and W7 breakout entries (10). Another 85 setups against a fixed −$10 stop: B2 and B3.
- **TP first** is the share of decided trades that reached the target before the stop.
- **Break-even** is the share needed for zero net result after costs, (stop + cost) / (target + stop).
- **Net R** is the average result per trade, in units of the planned loss (1 R = $650).

## Answer

1. **No plan reaches its target before its stop often enough to make money with confidence.** Of the 66 structural-stop plans, none has a net-R 95% lower bound above zero at +$10, +$15 or +$20. At +$10, none reaches TP first above its break-even rate.
2. **The fixed −$10 stop rows that sit above break-even are what chance produces among this many trials.**
   - B3 tested 210 trials. Its family Reality Check gives p = 1.0, its probability of overfitting (PBO) is 0.73, and its best z is +1.89. The luckiest of 210 trials with no edge typically shows a z of about 2.8.
   - B2 tested 54 trials: Reality Check p = 1.0.
   - Later studies of the same ideas, the operator's level rules and confirmations (B16–B21), failed.
3. **The closest plan with a large sample is W7 E0:M15: market at the M15 breakout close, stop beyond the breakout candle.**
   - It reached +$10 first in 51.3% of 1,031 trades, against 52.7% needed.
   - It beats random entries with the same stop (47.8%; paired p = 0.004, Holm p = 0.04), but it is still 1.4 points below break-even after costs.
   - On its 1,057 decided ENTRY READY trades it breaks even at a round-trip cost of **$0.39 / oz or less**. The study's cost model assumes $0.81.
   - +$15 and +$20 are negative even at zero cost.
4. **The registered next step is B31b-W8**: quality-selected E0 / E1 entries at the broker's measured cost. Its decision cost must be fixed by a committed amendment before the run.
   - Source 1: MetaApi spread measurements. MetaApi is not connected; the store has no broker data.
   - Source 2: the operator's MT5 XAUUSD specification and commission.
   - Fallback: the W3 cost model after 3 Dec 2026.
5. **Gaps make the $650 planned loss exceedable.** The worst realised losses in these studies were $779 (E0:M15), $2,696 (W5 A0) and $6,061 (W5 B0.786 limit).
