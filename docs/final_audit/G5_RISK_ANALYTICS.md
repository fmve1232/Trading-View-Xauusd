# G5: tail risk, Monte Carlo drawdown and expectancy before and after costs

**Authority.** Operator mandate of 27 Nov 2026, G5 APPROVED. This page covers:

- CVaR (the average of the worst 5% of outcomes);
- the Monte Carlo 95th-percentile maximum drawdown, using resampling that keeps intraday and daily dependence;
- expectancy before cost, after modelled cost and after measured cost.

The third G5 item, parameter sensitivity, is a separate registered study on development data. Registration
`research/registrations/G5-SENS.json` was written before its code, and it is not reported here.

**Read-only.** The tool reads the forward ledgers and changes nothing; the engine, its freeze key and the ledgers are untouched.

## Method (`audit/tools/risk_analytics.py`)

**Data**
- **Groups.** Every arm and timeframe, kept apart by freeze key. Arm H6 carries the previous key.
- **Trades.** Closed trades as recorded in the ledger. Trades with a zero stop distance are excluded and counted.

**Expectancy**
- **Before cost:** `gross_pts / stop`.
- **After modelled cost:** the ledger's net `r`, using the engine's cost of record.
- **Modelled cost:** `cost_pts / stop`.
- **After measured cost:** not available, because there are no broker fills (G2).

**Intervals**
- Day-block bootstrap: whole trading days are resampled with replacement, and the per-trade mean is recomputed.
- This keeps the dependence between trades of the same day.

**Tail**
- VaR 5% is the k-th worst outcome; CVaR 5% (expected shortfall) is the mean of the k worst, with k = ceil(0.05 n).
- It is computed per trade (net R) and per trading day (the day's summed net R, over days with at least one exit).
- With fewer than 20 outcomes, k = 1, so CVaR is the single worst outcome. The evidence file says so.

**Drawdown**
- The observed maximum drawdown is the largest fall of cumulative net R from a running peak, in exit order. The start counts as a peak at 0.
- The Monte Carlo distribution of it uses a **stationary bootstrap over trading days** (Politis & Romano 1994):
  - blocks of consecutive days with geometric lengths of mean L, wrapping at the end;
  - each day's trades stay together and in their recorded order, which keeps intraday dependence;
  - consecutive days stay together inside a block, which keeps daily dependence.
- Each path has as many days as the record. L = 1, 2 and 5 days.
- The output gives the 50th / 95th / 99th percentiles. The 95th is also shown in $ at $650 per R.
  - $650 is the operator's planned maximum loss per trade including costs: **an assumption of the plan**, not a measured fill.

**Other settings**
- **Trading day:** the New York session day, starting at 17:00 New York, dated by the trade's exit.
- **Seeds:** the run seed, plus a stable hash of each group's key. Adding a group never changes another group's numbers; a re-run is byte-identical.

## Validation

- **Hand-worked tests.** `tests/test_risk_analytics.py` covers:
  - VaR and CVaR, including the k rule at n = 20, 30 and 40;
  - drawdown, including exit order against ledger order;
  - the session-day boundary in winter and summer time;
  - the block structure of the stationary bootstrap;
  - each day kept whole and in order;
  - the day-block mean;
  - a hand-made ledger: open trades, manifests and archives skipped, freeze keys kept apart, zero stops excluded;
  - reproducibility, and seeds by key;
  - the limitations written into the output.
- **Mutation check.** The run is in `docs/final_audit/evidence/g5_mutation_2026-11-27.txt`.
- **Cross-check.** On the H4 15m ledger the observed drawdown equals `audit/tools/forward_stats.py`'s, which computes it in ledger order. One position at a time per arm makes the two orders the same.

Limitations written into the evidence file:

- the forward record covers few weeks: tails and drawdowns are estimated from few days
- resampling cannot produce a regime the record does not contain: the Monte Carlo drawdown is a lower bound on what a longer record could show
- each arm and timeframe alone; combined exposure across arms is G6
- costs are the engine's model of record, not broker-measured (G2)
- descriptive: nothing here selects a parameter

## How to read it

- **CVaR and drawdowns** are per arm and timeframe. Each arm holds one position at a time.
  - Trading several arms or timeframes at once adds their exposures. That is G6, not this page.
- **Short record.** The forward record covers few trading days, so a 95th percentile from it is a lower bound.
  - Resampling those days cannot produce a market the record does not contain.
- **Costs and money.**
  - "After modelled cost" uses the engine's cost of record. Measured costs need the MT5 specification and fills (G2).
  - The $ column is R times $650: the size of the plan, not a measured account result.
- **Descriptive only.** Nothing here selects a parameter or changes the engine.

## Reproduce

```sh
git fetch origin market-data && mkdir st && git archive origin/market-data | tar -x -C st
python audit/tools/risk_analytics.py st --json out.json --sims 10000 --seed 20261127
python audit/tools/risk_analytics.py --doc docs/final_audit/evidence/g5_risk_analytics_2026-11-27.json docs/final_audit/G5_RISK_ANALYTICS.md --check
```

A later snapshot gives different numbers, because the ledgers grow. To reproduce exactly, you need the ledger state
whose SHA-256 is in the run record. The forward-ledger branch keeps it in its history.
