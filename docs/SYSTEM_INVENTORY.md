# XAUUSD Quantum — system inventory and verification (2026-11-23)

The operator asked, on 2026-11-23, for a list of every strategy, development step, value, feature, engine, probability,
theory and literature source behind the code. The list had to be rechecked so that every part works as required, every
value is connected and calibrated, and no dead variable or dead code remains.

This document is that list. Every value comes from the code: the full values appendix,
[`docs/inventory/PRODUCTION_CONSTANTS.md`](inventory/PRODUCTION_CONSTANTS.md), is generated by
`audit/tools/inventory.py`, and `tests/test_inventory.py` enforces it on every build. Detailed results stay in the
documents named below. This file indexes them and reports what was verified today.

## 3. Features and values

- **Engine inputs:** the Pine input defaults in `quantum/config.py`.
  - Indicators: ATR 14, ADX 14 (threshold 25), RSI 14.
  - Structure: internal pivot 2, swing pivot 5, structure window 8, trend threshold 60, displacement 2.5 × ATR.
  - Zones: equal-level tolerance 0.25 ATR, FVG life 30 bars, OB life 20 bars, liquidity proximity 1.5 ATR, round-number grid $50.
  - Risk and costs: risk 1%, point value 100, spread 0.3, slippage 0.2, commission $7 per lot, holdout ratio 0.20.
  - The full list and the freeze key are in the appendix.
  - These values are never tuned on this price history; changing one restarts the forward test.
- **Feature families** (`features.py`, `engine.py`):
  - trend: EMA 20 / 100 / 200;
  - volatility: ATR and regime;
  - liquidity pools: previous day / week / month high and low, equal highs and lows, round numbers;
  - structure: BOS / CHoCH / MSS, swing and internal pivots;
  - FVG and order blocks;
  - volume profile: 100 bars, 40 bins, POC, 70% value area, on COMEX GC volume as the proxy;
  - sessions and kill zones;
  - higher-timeframe scores (1H / 4H / 1D);
  - macro and cross-asset (DXY, US10Y, real yield, COT, display or gate);
  - the analog engine (historical matches);
  - climax, SMT and displacement.
- **Monitors' values:**
  - Breakouts: pivot 3 / 3, swings live 5 trading days, EQ tolerance max($0.50, 0.10 × ATR14), 12-bar range ≤ 1.5 ATR, displacement ≥ 1 ATR, acceptance 2 closes, watch 16 bars, freshness 20 minutes.
  - Sweeps: 96-bar (24 h) watch; +$10 / +$15 / +$20 targets.
  - Shadow entries: stop buffer 0.2 ATR, extension ≤ 1 ATR, room ≥ $10, cost ≤ 15% of the stop.
  - Every module's constants are in the appendix.
- **The operator's plan** (`notify.plan_cfg`): $650 at the stop including the round-trip cost, sized from
  `ACCOUNT_USD` × `PLAN_RISK_PCT` (defaults $25,000 × 2.6%). TP1 is +$13 and TP2 +$20 at the 0.5-lot geometry. The
  cost is $26 per lot round trip, or the research cost model if that is higher. Neither cost is broker-verified until
  the MT5 specification and commission are entered.

Four other documents add to it:
- `16_CONFIRMED_TRADE_LITERATURE.md`: the drift that a 90% hit rate at 1:1 would need, and documented gold effects.
- `ENTRY_PRICE_RESEARCH.md`: optional stopping, and why no entry price confirms +$10.

The method rules the code follows come from that literature:
- development / validation / holdout partitions, with the holdout sealed;
- registration before code;
- Holm and Reality Check corrections;
- day-clustered bootstrap intervals;
- matched random-entry controls;
- Wilson intervals;
- no tuning on observed results.

## 9. Honest status

The system detects and reports sweeps and breakouts on M15, M30, H1 and H4 on time, at MT5 prices, with the
+$10 / +$15 / +$20 levels and a record of what happened next. It does **not** know in advance which sweep or breakout
will reach +$10 before its invalidation. Every study above found no rule that does so more often than random entries
after costs, so every message says "Entry: Not validated".

100% accuracy is not reachable: price gaps alone can exceed the planned stop. In the W7 data the worst gap loss was
$2,922 against the $650 planned.

What could change this verdict:
- the W8 run, once the broker's real cost is known;
- the live records reaching their registered checks: 30 for B15b, and 100 decided per timeframe for the sweep and breakout records.
